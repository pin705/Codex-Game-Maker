# Game Studio Business

Turn creative scope into a falsifiable product thesis before expensive production.

## Required Outputs

Create:

- `business/product-brief.md` from `../../references/templates/product-business-brief.md`
- `business/monetization-design.md` from `../../references/templates/monetization-design.md` when revenue is not `free-no-monetization`

## Workflow

1. Define audience, need/fantasy, comparable products, differentiator, target platforms, session model, and price/revenue model.
2. Research current comparable products and platform rules from primary sources. Record URLs and access dates; label assumptions separately from evidence.
3. Set production budget, schedule envelope, content boundary, minimum viable quality, break-even assumptions, and kill/pivot criteria.
4. Design monetization around player trust. Document entitlements, refunds, regional pricing, parental safeguards, currency sinks/sources, and anti-exploitation rules when applicable.
5. Connect commercial promises to the player-ready contract and marketing claims. Do not market features outside verified scope.
6. Revisit the plan after vertical-slice playtests and before content lock.

## Gate Rules

- A commercial release needs an approved product brief with evidence-backed positioning and explicit assumptions.
- Paid, ad-supported, DLC, subscription, or IAP games need an approved monetization design and platform-compliance owner.
- Revenue forecasts are scenarios, never guarantees.
- Do not approve manipulative dark patterns, pay-to-remove-friction designed into the base loop, undisclosed odds, or child-directed monetization without specialist review.
